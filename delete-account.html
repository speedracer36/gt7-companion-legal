Delete Your Account — GT7 Tuning Garage

How to delete your GT7 Tuning Garage account and the data tied to it

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app (immediate)

  1. Open GT7 Tuning Garage and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and can't be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app or can't sign in, email speedracer36@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my GT7 Tuning Garage account." We'll delete the account and confirm by reply, normally within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address, or your Apple sign-in link)
  • Your profile: display name and bio
  • Your Garage and Wanted lists
  • Tuning setups you created
  • Your block list and content reports you submitted

What is kept

  • Purchase records. If you bought the "Unlock All Tunes" purchase, the transaction record is held by Google Play or Apple and by our purchase-management provider, RevenueCat, for billing and tax purposes. We can't erase those from the app; deleting your account does not refund a purchase.
  • Crash logs. Anonymous crash reports held by our crash-reporting provider, Sentry, are not linked to your account and expire on their standard schedule.
