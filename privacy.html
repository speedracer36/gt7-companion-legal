Privacy Policy — GT7 Tuning Garage

Last updated: 2026-09-09

GT7 Tuning Garage ("the app", "we", "us") is an unofficial, fan-made companion app for Gran Turismo 7. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Interactive Entertainment or Polyphony Digital. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you use to sign up. Passwords are handled entirely by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and are never visible to us in plain text.
  • Display name and bio: optional, set by you in Settings. Shown on your profile within the app.
  • App content you create: which cars you've added to your Garage or Wanted list, and any tuning setups you build (name, description, use case, target PP, and the tuning settings themselves).
  • Moderation and safety data: if you report a tune, we store the report (which tune, your account, and your stated reason) for review. If you block another user, we store that block so their content can be filtered out of your view.

Tuning setups you create are visible to every user of the app, shown alongside your display name — that's the point of the community tuning feature. Other users can tap your name to see a mini profile (display name, bio, and how many tunes you've published). Your email address is never shown to other users.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera access, or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes (for example, adding crash reporting to fix bugs faster), this policy will be updated to say so before it happens.

How your data is used

Solely to provide the app's functionality: authenticating you, storing your Garage/Wanted lists and tuning setups, showing you the shared car catalog and community tunes, and letting you moderate what you see (reporting, blocking). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

App data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, on hosted infrastructure in the United States. Supabase processes data on our behalf under its own privacy and security practices.

In-app purchases

If the app offers a paid unlock, purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play — we do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, to unlock the corresponding features on your account.

Your choices

You can edit or clear your display name and bio at any time in Settings, and review or remove blocked users from Settings → Blocked Users. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data — your profile, Garage, Wanted list, and tuning setups — at any time via Settings → Delete Account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

GT7 Tuning Garage is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
