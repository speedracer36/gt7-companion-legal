Common questions

What is GT7 Tuning Garage?

An unofficial companion app for Gran Turismo 7. It gives you a searchable database of the full GT7 car catalog, lets you track which cars you own and which you're saving up for, and lets you build and share tuning setups with other players.

How do I add a car to my Garage or Wanted list?

Open any car from the Cars tab and tap "Add to Garage" (cars you already own in GT7) or "Add to Wanted" (cars you're saving credits for). Your Garage has its own tab; your Wanted list is reached from the Cars tab's Category filter — look for "⭐ Wanted List" at the top of the list.

How do tuning setups work, and what does the $2.99 unlock cover?

Every tune's name, use case, and PP level are visible to browse for free. The one-time $2.99 unlock covers two things: viewing the full tuning values (suspension, gearing, aero, and so on) for tunes other players have shared, and creating/sharing your own tuning setups. The car database, Garage, Wanted list, and GT News are free for everyone, no unlock needed.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the paywall (Settings → Unlock All Tunes, or tap any locked tune) and tap "Restore Purchases." Purchases are tied to your account, not a specific device.

How do I report a tune or block another user?

On any tune you didn't create, tap "⚑ Report" to flag it, or tap the creator's name to open their profile and block them. Blocking hides their tunes from your own view; it doesn't notify them. Reports are reviewed by a real person, not just collected.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, Garage, Wanted list, and tuning setups. It's immediate and can't be undone. Can't sign in? See how to request deletion by email.

Where does the car data come from?

The catalog is built from public GT7 reference sources and cross-checked for accuracy. Tuning setups are entirely user-submitted.
